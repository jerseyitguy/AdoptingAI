Theory & Understanding of AI

Artificial Intelligence (AI) means computers doing tasks that normally require human intelligence. Generative AI works a bit like a sophisticated predictive text engine. It doesn't retrieve facts from a database; instead, it has been trained on massive amounts of text to recognize patterns of language. It predicts the most likely next words to form a response.
